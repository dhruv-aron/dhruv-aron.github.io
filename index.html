About

Undergraduate senior at the University of Wisconsin-Madison majoring in Computer Science (with Honors). I have the great fortune of being advised by both Dr. Xiangyao Yu and Dr. Remzi Arpaci-Dusseau.

I am broadly interested in operating systems, distributed systems, databases, and AI/ML systems, with my research interests focusing on how to design systems infrastructure to better facilitate big data and AI/ML workloads. I was previously a Software Engineering Intern on the Lakebase team at Databricks, where I was advised by Dr. Haoyu Huang.
